2. Scope and app functions

This policy covers the Price Calculator Android app, Android package com.animadev.calcoloprezzi. The app requires no registration, creates no user account, offers no purchases, contains no advertising, and neither monetises nor sells data.

Price Calculator is a utility for price, percentage, VAT and margin calculations. It includes a mathematical loan simulation for information only: it does not offer financial services, grant loans, connect users with lenders or provide personalised financial advice.

3. Locally stored data

The app stores a small number of preferences only on the device, in the app’s local storage, for operation and personalisation:

  • language;
  • theme;
  • displayed currency;
  • onboarding status;
  • consent status for statistics, once this feature is implemented.

These preferences are not sent to AnimaDev. They remain on the device and can be removed by clearing the app’s data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

4. Future optional statistics

A future version may integrate Google Analytics for Firebase as its only Analytics service. Collection will be disabled by default and will begin only after explicit consent, with no collection beforehand. No option will be preselected. Refusal will not restrict the app; the choice can be changed in Settings and, after withdrawal, no new Analytics events will be sent.

The planned configuration does not include Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Ads, advertising, Google Signals, custom User-IDs, user-provided data or advertising personalisation. The Advertising ID will be disabled, as will granular collection of city and device details.

5. Data processed after consent

Standard automatically collected events

Firebase Analytics may process standard events automatically generated by the SDK relating to first launch, app launch or use, sessions, engagement in the app, app update and version, and the technical screen or activity class when generated automatically by the SDK.

Limited technical information

The operating system and its general version, app version, device or app language when made available by the SDK, platform, a pseudonymous app-instance identifier and approximate country may be processed. This data is not completely anonymous: it is pseudonymous or associated with a random installation identifier.

Custom events and parameter

Only three events will be configured: calculator_opened, calculate_pressed and calculation_completed. The only custom parameter will be calculator_id, with one of these values:

  • discount_price, discount_percentage;
  • vat_remove, vat_add, vat_rate;
  • markup, margin, unit_price, sale_price;
  • consecutive_percentages, loan.

These identifiers show only which function was used, not entered values or calculation results.

6. Approximate country

Google may derive the country approximately from the IP address while providing the service. The app does not request Android location permission, access GPS or collect precise location. City is not collected through granular configuration, which will be disabled.

VPNs, corporate networks and network operators can make the country inaccurate. AnimaDev views aggregated statistics by country only. This description does not mean that Google never processes the IP address during technical transmission and processing.

7. Data excluded from custom events

AnimaDev does not configure custom events to send:

  • prices, percentages, VAT rates, margins, mark-ups, selected currency, quantities, calculation results or validation errors;
  • loan principal, rates, annual percentage rate (APR), loan term, dates, instalments or amortisation schedule;
  • typed text, name, user email, phone number, address, contacts, photos or files;
  • GPS location or Advertising ID.

8. Purposes

Optional statistics will be used solely to understand how often the app is used, measure general feature use, understand which calculators are opened and how often a calculation is requested and completed, improve usability, stability and development priorities, and assess general interest in the app.

They will not be used for advertising, commercial profiling, remarketing, sale of data, automated decisions, granting credit or financial assessment of users.

9. Legal basis and consent

For users subject to the GDPR, consent will be the legal basis for Firebase Analytics. Consent is optional, specific to statistics and separate from simply viewing the Privacy Policy. Acceptance of the policy will not be a condition for using the app.

No option will be preselected and refusal will not restrict features. The choice can be changed in Settings. Withdrawal will apply to future collection and stop new events being sent, but will not automatically delete data already transmitted and aggregated. Privacy requests can be sent to animadev.apps@gmail.com.

10. Recipients and providers

When Analytics is authorised, data will be processed through Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. Google operates under the terms applicable to its services and may use affiliated companies and international infrastructure to provide the service. AnimaDev does not directly control every server used by Google and cannot state that Google can never access the data.

Official information:

  • Privacy and data security in Firebase (Firebase);
  • Google Privacy Policy;
  • Google Analytics data safeguards;
  • Google Analytics data controls and deletion requests.

11. International transfers

Google may process data outside the user’s country and outside the European Economic Area. In that case, processing is subject to the safeguards provided for in Google’s terms and applicable law. The actual location of infrastructure may vary, and no absolute guarantee is given that data will remain in one country.

12. Retention

For the future Analytics property, event and user data retention will be set to 14 months and the option to reset the period on new activity will be disabled. This does not mean every item of data is deleted exactly after 14 months: aggregated reports may remain available for longer according to how Google Analytics works.

Local preferences remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Emails voluntarily sent to support are retained for as long as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations.

13. Rights and requests

Where provided by applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with the competent authority.

The app creates no account, and AnimaDev does not directly collect a name or email through the app. Analytics data is associated with pseudonymous identifiers; the absence of identifiers directly linked to a person may make it difficult to locate a specific installation. Technically impossible deletion is therefore not promised, but every request will still be assessed when sent to animadev.apps@gmail.com.

14. Children

The app is a general utility for price, percentage, VAT, margin and simulation calculations. It is not designed or promoted specifically for children, does not create profiles of children and does not knowingly collect personal data provided by children. A parent or guardian may contact AnimaDev with concerns.

15. Security

Measures include data minimisation, protections and encrypted channels provided by the platforms and Google, and restricted access to the project console. No system is entirely risk-free. Users should avoid emailing sensitive information to support unless it is necessary for the request.

16. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated because of app changes, new regulatory requirements or changes to services used. Material changes will be communicated in the app where appropriate. The last updated date is always shown at the start of this document.

17. Privacy of this website

This static website uses no cookies, Google Analytics, Firebase, advertising or forms, and records no data directly. When an email link is selected, the user’s email client handles sending. GitHub Pages and network infrastructure may nevertheless process ordinary technical data needed to deliver the site under their respective policies.
